## ⚡ Who I Am

Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Engineer, holding a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence, whose work lies at the intersection of mathematical modeling, intelligent systems design, and the deep understanding of the principles governing algorithmic behavior. His research-oriented approach views artificial intelligence as a scientific discipline, prioritizing formal understanding, computational performance, and interpretability over black-box solutions. His trajectory as a researcher and educator has focused on the analysis and development of artificial intelligence models from a rigorous and systemic perspective, integrating mathematical foundations, algorithmic thinking, and systems architecture. As a strong advocate of Linux—and Arch Linux in particular—he embraces a philosophy centered on low-level understanding, transparency, and full control of the computational environment. These principles directly inform his vision of AI: not merely understanding what models do, but why they do it and how they can be improved.
